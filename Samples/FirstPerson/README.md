# First Person (Basalt sample)

A small first-person scene: mouse look with a locked cursor, a character controller, raycast shooting, and the audio
effects (low-pass occlusion, reverb, Doppler), built only through the automation API (`build_scene.json`).

- **Player** — a capsule `CharacterController` (`Player.lua`). The body turns left and right; its `Camera` child (the
  primary camera and the `AudioListener`) tilts up and down, clamped at 85 degrees, so the capsule stays upright.
  The mouse turns the view through `Input.GetMouseDelta()` while the cursor is `Locked`; the right stick turns it
  too. Footsteps play from the player's `AudioSource` while it walks on the ground.
- **Shooting** — the left mouse button (or the right bumper) casts a ray from the camera (`Physics.Raycast`, ignoring
  the player) and plays the `Gun`'s shot. A hit target tips over, scores a point and stands back up after four
  seconds; a fallen target scores nothing.
- **Occlusion** — the `Generator` hums behind a wall. Every frame after physics, `Game.lua` casts a ray from the
  listener to it and sets its `AudioSource.LowPass` to 500 Hz while anything solid is in the way, 0 (off) when the
  line is clear: step out from behind the wall and its buzz brightens.
- **Reverb** — the stone hall has a trigger (`HallZone`). Entering it switches the reverb to a large, bright room
  (`Audio.SetReverb`) and raises the `ReverbSend` of the shots and footsteps, so they ring; leaving returns to the
  courtyard's small, dull room. The generator's small send stays fixed, so it rings with whichever room the player
  is in.
- **Doppler** — the `Drone` flies back and forth behind the targets with a rotor sound whose `AudioSource.Doppler`
  is 1: it sounds higher while it comes toward you and lower as it leaves. It is moved by script, so the audio system
  measures its velocity from its movement each frame (a physics body's velocity is used directly).
- **HUD** — UI text: the score, the area, a crosshair and a hint that changes when the mouse is released.

**Controls** are input actions (`Input.binput`, written by `build_scene.json` through `project.set inputActions`):

| Action | Bindings |
|--------|----------|
| `MoveX`, `MoveY` (axes) | WASD, the arrow keys, the left stick (dead zone 0.2) |
| `LookX`, `LookY` (axes) | the right stick (dead zone 0.15); the mouse turns the view directly |
| `Jump` | Space, the gamepad's A |
| `Fire` | the left mouse button, the right bumper |
| `Unlock` | Escape, Start: releases the mouse (a click takes it back without firing) |

In the editor, the Game view must have focus for the cursor to lock; the editor keeps Escape for itself and
releases the mouse when you press it.

Scripts (`Assets/Scripts`): `Player.lua` (look, walk, shoot), `Game.lua` (targets, occlusion, areas, HUD) and
`Drone.lua`. The sounds are generated by `scripts/generate_sample_assets.py`; the font is Karla (SIL Open Font
License, `Assets/Fonts/Karla-OFL.txt`).

Play: `build/bin/BasaltRuntime --project Samples/FirstPerson`.
Rebuild the scene: `build/bin/basalt --project Samples/FirstPerson batch Samples/FirstPerson/build_scene.json`.

## The gameplay test

`test_game.json` (run by ctest as `SampleFirstPerson`) runs headless. Look results are checked through the camera's
forward vector and with tolerances, since trigonometry differs slightly between platforms; only the pitch clamp,
which is exact, is compared exactly.

- checks the scene before play (the saved scene matches, the listener is on the camera, the drone's Doppler is on,
  the hall's zone is a trigger) and the state after the first frames: the cursor is locked, the seven actions
  exist, the player stands, the wall hides the generator (`LowPass` 500) and the courtyard reverb is set;
- turns the view with the mouse (the first `input.mouse` position only seeds the previous one): right, down, past
  the pitch limit (it stops at 85 degrees), up, and level again;
- shoots the middle target (score 1, the HUD and the log agree), aims down at it while it lies on the ground and
  shoots it again (no score), aims 16 degrees left with the mouse and shoots the left target;
- turns with a gamepad's right stick (nothing inside the dead zone, 140 degrees per second at full tilt);
- jumps and lands;
- strafes out from behind the wall (footsteps play; the generator's low-pass turns off) and back (it turns on again),
  then shoots the middle target, which has stood back up;
- walks into the hall (the trigger fires, the reverb and sends change, the hall walls hide the generator) and out;
- samples the drone over a full sweep: each Doppler pitch matches 343.3 / (343.3 - closing speed), higher while it
  approaches and lower while it leaves;
- releases the mouse with Escape (the view stops turning, the hint changes) and takes it back with a click that does
  not fire; and finally checks that stopping restores the edit scene.
