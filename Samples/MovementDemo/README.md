# Movement Demo (Basalt sample)

A mannequin walking an obstacle course, showing animation layers and inverse kinematics working together. The scene
is built only through the automation API (`build_scene.json`):

- **Walk** — the base clip, driven by a `CharacterController` that `Walker.lua` moves back and forth at the clip's
  stride speed, over a flight of stairs, a platform, a ramp and a few stones.
- **Foot placement** — two `Ground` chains (`InverseKinematics`) cast a ray under each foot, keep it at its animated
  height above whatever it lands on, lower the `Hips` for the lower foot and turn the feet to the surface.
- **Look-at** — the head, neck and chest (`ChainLength` 3) follow a glowing orb, up to 80 degrees from the animated
  aim.
- **Wave** — an `Override` layer masked to the right arm fades in every seven seconds (`SetAnimationLayerWeight`)
  while the legs keep walking.
- **Breathing** — an `Additive` layer lifts the chest on top of everything.
- **Reach** — a `TwoBone` chain on the left arm follows the orb (`SetIKTarget` with an entity) and fades in when the
  orb swoops close (`SetIKWeight`).

`Orb.lua` circles the orb around the walker's head; `FollowCamera.lua` follows from the front-side.

The mannequin (`Assets/Models/Mannequin.gltf`) is generated by `GenerateMannequin.py`: boxes rigidly skinned to a
human-proportioned skeleton, with `Walk`, `Wave` and `Breathe` clips.

Play: `build/bin/BasaltRuntime --project Samples/MovementDemo`.
Rebuild the scene: `build/bin/basalt --project Samples/MovementDemo batch Samples/MovementDemo/build_scene.json`.

## The gameplay test

`test_game.json` (run by ctest as `SampleMovementDemo`) plays the walk and checks it at four points:

- At the stairs: the lower (stance) foot stands on the ground under it (raycast), no foot sinks into it, breathing
  is on, and the reaching arm points at the orb.
- On the platform: the walker climbed it, its stance foot stands on the platform, and the masked wave raised the
  right hand above the head without moving the legs.
- Past the ramp: the wave faded out and the stance foot is on the ground again.
- At the end of the course: the walker turned around.

Every check also verifies the look-at: when the orb is within 60 degrees of where the body faces, the head aims at it
within a degree; otherwise it turned at least partly toward it.
